Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

This policy explains how the Society for Paediatric Anaesthesia in New Zealand and Australia (SPANZA) handles personal information through Journal Watch, at journalwatch.org.au and in the Journal Watch newsletter. In this policy, “we” means SPANZA Journal Watch.

Information we collect

We collect personal information when you subscribe to the newsletter, create an account, contribute a review or write to us. We also record how readers use the site and the newsletter. We do not collect payment details, we do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for marketing. Journal Watch makes no decisions about you by automated means.

Information How we collect it Why we use it How long we keep it
Your email address and subscription status When you subscribe, create an account, or are added from a SPANZA membership list To send the newsletter and to let you sign in While you are subscribed or hold an account. After you unsubscribe, we keep your address only so that we do not email you again.
Your name When you add it to your account To address you correctly and to show it on your CPD reports Until you remove it or close your account
Reading activity: the pages you read, time on each page, searches, the site that referred you and your browser type Automatically, as you use the site To learn which reviews readers find useful and to improve the site 24 months
Newsletter opens and link clicks Automatically, when your email program loads the newsletter’s images or you follow one of its links To learn which issues and reviews readers find useful 24 months
Your CPD reading record: the articles you open in full text, with dates Only while CPD tracking is turned on in your account To produce your CPD reports Until you close your account
CPD reports When you request one To give you a record of your reading 30 days
Enquiries you send us When you email Journal Watch To reply to you 24 months
Contributor details: title, name, email address, health service and photograph When you agree to contribute a review To run the editorial process and to credit you on published reviews, unless you have asked to be anonymous While your reviews are published, or until you delete your account

Reading activity is recorded against a random visitor identifier, not against your name or email address. When you follow a link in the newsletter, we record that click against your subscription.

Cookies

Journal Watch uses a small number of cookies, and none of them is used for advertising. No third-party tracking scripts run on the site.

Cookie Purpose How long it lasts
sessionid Keeps you signed in and holds your choices during a visit Two weeks, or 12 hours for editorial staff
csrftoken Protects the site’s forms from misuse by other websites One year
jwvid Counts visits and reading with a random identifier One year
jw_sub Records that this browser has subscribed, so the site does not keep inviting you to subscribe One year
messages Carries a one-off confirmation message to the next page Until the message is shown

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with the service providers that run Journal Watch for us, and only as far as each service requires. Each provider handles the information on our behalf.

Provider Service Where the information is held
Our hosting provider Runs the server that holds the database, the site and our editorial tools Los Angeles, United States
Amazon Web Services Sends our email, and stores images, incoming email and encrypted backups Sydney, Australia
Cloudflare Delivers the website securely, and handles every visit to it A global network operated from the United States
Sentry Receives a report when the site fails, without names, email addresses or form contents United States

Because our main server is in the United States, the personal information in our database is stored there. Cloudflare and Sentry are also based in the United States. We remain responsible for how these providers handle your information, and we choose providers that encrypt information in transit and restrict who can reach it.

Security and your choices

All connections to Journal Watch use HTTPS. Only editorial staff with the relevant permission can see subscriber lists, reading statistics or enquiries. Our backups are encrypted before they leave the server.

You can read Journal Watch without an account or a subscription. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link at the foot of every newsletter. You can turn CPD tracking on or off in your account.

You can delete your account from the Account panel. Reviews you have written stay published, credited to Anonymous.

If an account is not confirmed within seven days, we delete it.

Access, correction and complaints

You have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and to ask us to correct it if you think it is wrong. You can also ask us to delete your account and your subscription. To make a request, email journalwatch@spanza.org.au or write to SPANZA Journal Watch, PO Box 44, Gosford NSW 2250, Australia. We will reply within 30 days.

If you are concerned about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first at the same address, and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner or, in New Zealand, to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our handling of personal information changes. The date at the top of the policy shows when it last changed.
