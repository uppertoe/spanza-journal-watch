Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

This policy explains how the Society for Paediatric Anaesthesia in New Zealand and Australia (SPANZA) handles personal information collected through Journal Watch, both on journalwatch.org.au and in the Journal Watch newsletter. In this policy, “we” means SPANZA Journal Watch.

Information we collect

We collect personal information when you subscribe to the newsletter, create an account, contribute a review or write to us, and we record how readers use the site and the newsletter. We do not collect payment details, and we do not sell or share personal information. We make no decisions about you by automated means.

Information How we collect it Why we use it How long we keep it
Your email address and subscription status When you subscribe, create an account or are added from a SPANZA membership list To send you the newsletter and to let you sign in While you are subscribed or hold an account. After you unsubscribe, we keep the address only so that we do not email you again.
Your name When you add it to your account To address you correctly and to print it on your CPD reports Until you remove it or delete your account
Your reading list: the articles you save, mark as read or recommend for review When you use these features while signed in To keep your reading list on every device, and to show the editors which articles readers recommend Until you delete your account
Reading activity: the pages you read, how long you spend and how far you scroll on each, your searches, the website that referred you and your browser type Automatically, as you use the site To learn which reviews readers find useful and to improve the site 24 months
Newsletter opens and link clicks Automatically, when your email program loads the newsletter’s images or you follow one of its links To learn which issues and reviews readers find useful 24 months
Your CPD reading record: the articles you open in full text, with the date of each Only while CPD tracking is turned on in your account To produce your CPD reports Until you delete your account
CPD reports When you request one To give you a record of your reading 30 days
Messages you send us When you email Journal Watch To reply to you 24 months
Contributor details: title, name, email address, health service and photograph When you agree to contribute a review To run the editorial process and to credit you on published reviews, unless you have asked to be anonymous While your reviews are published, or until you delete your account. Your name and email address remain in our editorial board system until an administrator removes them.

Your reading activity is recorded against a random visitor identifier rather than your name or email address. A click on a link in the newsletter is recorded against your subscription.

Cookies

Journal Watch sets the five cookies below. None of them is used for advertising, and no third-party tracking scripts run on the site.

Cookie What it does How long it lasts
sessionid Keeps you signed in and remembers your choices during a visit Two weeks, or 12 hours for editorial staff
csrftoken Protects the site’s forms from misuse by other websites One year
jwvid Holds the random identifier used to count visits and reading One year
jw_sub Records that you have subscribed from this browser, so that the site stops inviting you to subscribe One year
messages Carries a confirmation message to the next page you open Until the message is shown

Where your information is stored

We do not share your personal information with anyone. Journal Watch runs on the services below, which store or carry information for us and do not use it for their own purposes.

Service What it does for Journal Watch Location
Our hosting provider Runs the server that holds our database, the website and our editorial tools Los Angeles, United States
Amazon Web Services Sends our email, and stores images, incoming email and our encrypted backups Sydney, Australia
Cloudflare Delivers the website securely to your browser A global network run from the United States
Sentry Receives a report when the site fails, with names, email addresses and form contents removed United States

Because our main server is in the United States, the personal information in our database is held there. We remain responsible for your information while these services hold it.

Security and your choices

All connections to Journal Watch use HTTPS. Only editorial staff who hold the relevant permission can see subscriber lists, reading statistics or messages sent to us, and our backups are encrypted before they leave the server.

You can read Journal Watch without an account or a subscription. You can unsubscribe with the link at the foot of every newsletter, and you can turn CPD tracking on or off in your account.

You can delete your account from the Account panel. Reviews you have written stay published, credited to Anonymous. If a new account is not confirmed within seven days, we delete it.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and your subscription. To make a request, email journalwatch@spanza.org.au or write to SPANZA Journal Watch, PO Box 44, Gosford NSW 2250, Australia. We will reply within 30 days.

If you are concerned about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first at the same address. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner or, in New Zealand, to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy whenever our handling of personal information changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
