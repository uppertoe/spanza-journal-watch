Regional Coordinator Guide
==========================

When the chief editor assigns you an issue, three things fall to you:
shortlisting the articles, inviting the reviewers, and editing the finished
reviews. The chief editor then publishes the issue. The rest of this page is
a quick reminder of where the buttons are.

How it fits together
--------------------

.. container:: feature

   .. container:: feature-text

      1. The platform pulls the articles for the issue's window from PubMed
         and applies the paediatric filter.
      2. You pick out the articles worth reviewing and send them across to
         the Planka board.
      3. Reviewers claim articles on the board and write their reviews
         there.
      4. You pull the finished reviews into Journal Watch, edit them, and
         mark them publish ready for the chief editor.

   .. raw:: html
      :file: _demos/overview.html


Logging in
----------

.. container:: feature flip

   .. container:: feature-text

      1. Go to `/editorial/go </editorial/go>`_ and sign in. Once you are
         signed in, the same page is available under **Editor** in the
         account menu on the main site.
      2. Choose **Editorial backend**.
      3. Your dashboard lists the issues you have been assigned. Open one
         with **Articles** or **Reviewers**.

      If the dashboard is empty, you have not been assigned an issue yet, so
      ask the chief editor to add you.

   .. raw:: html
      :file: _demos/go.html


Shortlisting articles
---------------------

Open **Articles** for the issue. Load the articles once, then work down the
list: shortlist what is worth reviewing, and send the shortlist to the board
from the bar at the bottom of the page.

Load the articles
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. container:: feature

   .. container:: feature-text

      1. Set **From month** and **To month** to the issue's window.
      2. Tick the journals you want, or use **Select all**.
      3. Click **Load the articles**. The list appears within a few seconds,
         and PubMed is checked for anything newer in the background.

      Once the list is loaded, the months and journals fold into the strip
      at the top of the page. **Change range or journals** opens them again.

   .. raw:: html
      :file: _demos/load.html

Keep the list up to date
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. container:: feature flip

   .. container:: feature-text

      Articles keep arriving in PubMed for weeks after they are published,
      so the list is not complete until a fortnight or so after the window
      closes.

      1. Click **Check for new articles** on the strip at the end of each
         month in the window, and once more a fortnight after it closes.
         The strip turns amber when a check is overdue.
      2. New arrivals are marked with a blue dot. Under **Filters**, tick
         **New only** to see just those, and choose **Mark all seen** from
         the **More** menu once you have looked through them.

      Anything you have already shortlisted or sent is left exactly as it
      was.

   .. raw:: html
      :file: _demos/recheck.html

Shortlist the articles
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. container:: feature

   .. container:: feature-text

      The **Paediatric** filter is on when the page opens. Switch it off if
      you want to see everything. The first lines of each abstract sit under
      the title, and **More** opens the rest.

      1. Click **Shortlist** on each article you want reviewed. The button
         turns green and reads *Shortlisted*. Click it again to take the
         article off.
      2. More articles load as you scroll. The search box, the journal menu
         and **Filters** narrow the list.
      3. For an article that is not in the list, click **Add an article**
         and paste a DOI or PMID, or type part of the title.

      **Shortlist**, at the top of the list, shows only what you have
      picked. Shortlisting changes nothing on the public website or on the
      board.

   .. raw:: html
      :file: _demos/stage.html

Send the shortlist to the review board
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. container:: feature flip

   .. container:: feature-text

      The bar at the bottom of the page counts the shortlisted articles that
      are not on the board yet, and stays amber until they are sent.

      1. Click **Send to Planka**.
      2. Each shortlisted article becomes a card in the *Candidates* list on
         the board, and the bar turns green.

      You can shortlist more and send again whenever you like. Once the
      board is up to date the button reads **Send again**: it makes no
      duplicates, and only replaces a card that has gone missing. If the
      issue has no board yet, the bar says so and links to the Planka
      setup. **Re-check the board**, on the Shortlist view, shows which
      cards the reviewers have moved or removed.

   .. raw:: html
      :file: _demos/push.html


Inviting reviewers
------------------

Open **Reviewers** for the issue.

.. container:: feature

   .. container:: feature-text

      1. Enter the reviewer's name and email address and click **Add**.
      2. Click **Send invite** on the bar above the table. The bar shows how
         many reviewers have been added but not yet invited. The links stay
         valid for 180 days.
      3. *Not invited* means the reviewer has been added but no email has
         gone out. *Invited* means the email has gone out. *Active* means
         the reviewer has accepted and is on the board.
      4. To send a fresh link, tick the reviewer's row and click **Resend to
         selected**. The earlier link stops working. **Revoke** removes
         someone from the issue.

   .. raw:: html
      :file: _demos/invite.html


Working in Planka
-----------------

On the Planka login page, click **Sign in with Journal Watch**. You are a
member of the board for every issue you coordinate, so you can move cards
between lists, comment on them, and see at a glance where each review is up
to.

Comments on a card are for discussion. Make any edits to a review in Journal
Watch after the card has been pulled in.


Editing the reviews
-------------------

Reviewers move their finished cards to *Ready to submit* on the board.

1. Open **Pull Reviews** for the issue. Tick the cards you want and click
   **Import selected**, or click **Import all from Ready to submit**.
2. Open **Edit Reviews**. Click **Edit** on a review to change its text,
   author or article, then click **Update review**.
3. When a review needs no more work, click **Mark publish ready**. To mark
   several at once, tick them and click **Mark selected publish ready**.
   **Unmark** clears the mark.

Most reviews need no editing, so they can be marked publish ready as soon as
they are pulled in. The chief editor publishes the issue once the reviews
are marked.
